What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence under the definition of Nasa, refers to it as an artificial system that can adapt and learn, being able to do a large variety of tasks with efficiency. Artificial Intelligence is taught to act and think like a human, as a result can help aid in tasks that normally humans can only do with efficiency and little to no human supervision. As a result, Ai is not only just a system that follows a sense of instructions, but is also able to think rationally enabling them to make their own decisions and problem solve.

02

How does it work?

Artificial Intelligence works by collecting massive amounts of data in the world wide web, learning from all the different information, as a result they can develop patterns and learn to generate answers based on the millions of the information gathered in the internet, do not mistake AI as an algorithm following a million instructions, it's better to understand it as giving a machine a billion scenarios worth of information to produce a result.

03

What are examples of GenAI tools?

Generative AI can produce many different products that normally can only be done by humans. The most common example would be generation of text in AI. Other examples include Imagery AI, creating images and videos with the use of entirely AI. Another would be Ai being able to generate code, enabling the creation of websites and apps in just minutes.

04

Difference between Generative Ai, and Artificial intelligence

Based on Amazon, Artificial Intelligence is the larger broader scope, while Generative Ai is just another branch of AI which enables the creation of content in an intelligent way.

05

What can GenAI do?

Generative AI can make content and ideas, having the ability to learn human language, physics, biology, architecture, etc. Essentially being able to learn tasks that humans can only normally do, and with ruthless effeciency at that.

What are some of its implementations and risks?

Some of AI's risks would be its ethical concerns, with one ethical concern being the information taken in by the AI, if its unbaised or even factual information. Another concern would be the method in which AI collects data, and if th data collected may violate any privacy concerns. Lastly in the context of education we cannot ignore the quality of education that students will experience since they might abuse the use of ai in their learning.
