Ethics of Artificial Intelligence

Part 1 : Introduction

Artificial Intelligence continues to rise, with this tool being integrated more into our day to day lives, this leads to a rise in the question of ethics, and when do we draw the line in using this tool, and what does this imply the future of how we go about solving problems in the future?

Part 2 : Plagiarism and Misuse

With how powerful Artificial Intelligence is, specifically in the topic of generative AI, no doubt will there be a rise of misuse of this said tool. Misuse include Academic integrity which will be discussed further, and plagiarism will no doubt be a pravalent issue. As AI gathers information from the internet, it may use articles and different websites as reference in creating its response. This raises the concern of the possibility of AI plagiarising an author's work.

Part 3 : Responsibility in Academics

Artificial Intelligence has no doubt affected the education of students, in a way that hinders their academic growth by letting them rely on artificial intelligence to do their tasks for them. A shift of if the student is plagirising to if the student is using AI is becoming much more common than not. This however raises the concern on how to detect the use of AI in academic works, with most AI detectors being inaccurate at times, a study in MIT confirms this fact. So this goes back to problem with AI, while a great tool for the advancement of our civilization, can also lead to very big problems in terms of academics. As such a solution is needed in order to combat this issue at hand.

Part 4 : Accuracy and Verification

An article created by MIT(2025) states that, " AI detection software is far from foolproof—in fact, it has high error rates and can lead instructors to falsely accuse students of misconduct." This makes checking AI quite hard to predict and As AI grows at an exponential rate, we can further imply that "Ai-checkers" will become more and more unreliable. Furthermore, it is important that we also discuss the problem in regards to the accuracy of AI in giving its information. As AI gets its information from the internet, and that it is safe to assume that there is a large amount of inaccurate data floating in the world wide web, how can we sure that what AI says is truly accurate and not false. On a good note, as AI gets smarter and smarter, we can also say that AI will be able to distinguish which data is true and which one is false.

Part 5 : Bias and Fairness

As artificial intelligence is being used in various kinds of fields, its important we discuss the problems of bias and fairness of AI. An example would be the use of AI in terms of job applicants, if the AI algorithm in data collection is not diverse, this may lead to AI being biased in its filtering of job applicants, say AI filtering out female applicants. Making unbiased data collection with AI is difficult to do as AI gathers information from the internet which has a lot of biased information, making it difficult to create a fair AI.

Part 6 : Recommended Guidelines

• When handling any branch of Artificial Intelligence, it is crucial that as responsible netizens we use this tool with proper boundaries and guidelines.

• Do not make AI your main source of information. While AI is rapidly improving, it can still generate inaccuracies and cannot be held liable for misinformation.

• Avoid over-reliance on AI for academic work. Excessive dependence hinders true learning, weakens critical thinking skills, and reduces your understanding of the subject.
