B. Analogy on the Use of AI in Dev Work

Imagine you are organizing your wedding, and the documentation team offers a ₱40,000 package. They will use an iPhone 16 Pro Max to cover the event and CapCut for the same-day edit video. The team only has 2 years of experience, although their portfolio is above average. The question is: would you accept this deal? Explain your answer and justify the integrity of their work. Reflect on the integrity, creativity, skill level of the “vibe” coders.

Reflection:

The answer in whether I decide to accept this deal or not comes with many major factors. It raises the question, does the skill justify the price? That depends, and furthermore does the simple equipement make their service less desirable? When deciding its important to say that we cannot judge one's skill level based on the level of gear that they have because the quality of equipement does not corrolate with the level of skill, say a professional badminton player plays with a very cheap racket and a casual player playing with a top of the line racket, 10 times of 10 the professional player will beat the recreational player. However its also safe to assimilate that the price of such service does not automatically mean that the quality of service is excellent. Ultimately when deciding we must know if the quality of service matches the standards you have set. In my case I'd take the deal if the quality of service matches my standards even despite the simple equipement.

Analogy on the Use of AI in Dev Work

This analogy can be used in the same context with the new "vibe" coders as they say. Vibe coders being coders who don't really code persay but lets tools such as AI do the work for them. This raises the question if people use AI to do their work, does this make their work any less valuable than if it was done manually? In the simplest form, what makes a good coder isn't the tools it has, but a person's understanding of said tools and how to use them to the fullest. Like how we can't say that the quality of the output depends on the tools they have, we can also say that the use of AI does not necessarily mean that the output of said coder will be subpar. However in order to use these tools to the fullest, you must have the skills to back it up, just like how an artist can create art using the simplest of things, can a coder create this output wthout the use of AI? Do they have the problem solving skills to back it up? These are all the questions you must ask te so called vibe coders, because while the use of AI is not necessarily discouraged, they must have the ability to be able to do these tasks without this tool.
